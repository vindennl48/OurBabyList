Must-Have

Before She Comes Home

Check these off first. "Buy new" is a hard rule for the two safety items — car seat and mattress — and anything else you can't verify isn't recalled.

01 Car Seat & the Ride Home

The only item you legally can't leave the hospital without.
  • Infant car seatBuy new

    Rear-facing, back seat, middle position. No puffy coats or snowsuits under the harness.

    Budget Graco SnugRide SnugFit 35 — $160 Spend Chicco KeyFit Max ClearTex — $250
  • Second base (if two cars)Buy new

    Optional — skip if you drive one car.

    Optional — $85
  • Going-home outfit + blanketUsed OK

    Blanket goes over the buckled harness, never under it.

    Est. — $25

02 Sleep

Room-share for the first 6 months. Bare, flat, firm — nothing else in the sleep space.
  • Bassinet (room-share)Used OK · new mattress

    Avoid the Halo BassiNest Flex Portable (recall petition). Firm, flat, mesh sides.

    Budget Baby Delight Beside Me Doze Deluxe — $96 Spend Halo BassiNest Swivel Sleeper 3.0 — $220
  • Fitted sheets + waterproof protectorBuy new

    2–3 of each, in both bassinet and crib sizes. 3 a.m. blowouts happen.

    Est. — $50
  • Swaddles / sleep sacks (3–5)Buy new

    NOT weighted. One TOG 2.5 for winter + a couple lighter. Zip/arms-up is quieter than Velcro at 3 a.m.

    Est. — $50–80
  • Pacifiers (2+)Buy new

    One-piece, shield ≥1.5". Offer at sleep (lowers SIDS risk). If breastfeeding, wait ~3–4 weeks. Never clip/string it.

    Est. — $10

03 Feeding

  • Haakaa silicone collectorBuy new

    Est. — $15
  • Nursing bras (2–3)Buy new

    No underwire, one-hand clips. Buy a size up for engorgement.

    Est. — $60–90
  • Nursing pads + nipple creamBuy new

    Lanolin cream; disposable pads for the first weeks.

    Est. — $20
  • Bottles + slow-flow nipples (2–4)Buy new

    Get two different brands — she'll have a preference. Level 0/1 newborn nipples.

    Est. — $30–40
  • Bottle brush + drying rackBuy new

    Dedicated rack keeps pump parts separate from kitchen dishes.

    Est. — $15
  • Milk storage bagsFree

    Often included with your insurance pump (Aeroflow sends 400+).

    Cost — $0, else $15
  • Formula backup (2 oz ready-to-feed)Buy new

    12–24 oz total. Sterile, no mixing — perfect for a 1 a.m. emergency. Free via Enfamil/Similac samples or hospital.

    Est. — $15–20
  • Burp cloths (10–15)Used OK

    Buy thick terry, not thin muslin. The #1 "we forgot" item.

    Est. — $20–30

      Five Safety Rules

      Non-negotiable, straight from the AAP and CPSC.
      1. Buy new only: car seat and crib/bassinet mattress. Everything else can be used if you check cpsc.gov/Recalls first.
      2. She never sleeps in a swing, bouncer, lounger, car seat, or anything inclined over 10°. Flat, firm, bare surface only.
      3. Bare crib: fitted sheet + baby. No bumpers, pillows, loose blankets, positioners, or weighted swaddles/sleep sacks.
      4. Avoid the Halo BassiNest Flex Portable and any recalled swing/bouncer on the CPSC list.
      5. No OTC meds under ~3 months without the pediatrician's OK. Fever ≥100.4°F under 3 months = call the doctor, don't self-treat.
